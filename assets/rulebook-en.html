🎯 1. Game Objective

Mafia is a party game between three teams: Mafia, Citizens, and Independents. The game is managed by a "God" (moderator).

  • Mafia Team: Eliminates citizens and independents during the night.
  • Citizen Team: Tries to identify and eliminate mafia and independents.
  • Independents: Have their own separate goals.

The game alternates between night and day phases until one team meets their victory condition.

🏆 2. Victory Conditions

🔵 Citizen Victory — All mafia members (Godfather and all associates) AND all independents (Jack & Zodiac) are eliminated.

🔴 Mafia Victory — Alive mafia count is ≥ total of alive citizens + independents (unless exactly 3 remain with an independent → handshake).

🟣 Jack Instant Win — If all mafia die while Jack is still alive → Jack wins immediately, regardless of other players.

🟣 Zodiac Victory — Zodiac does NOT auto-win when mafia dies. Must keep killing until 3 players remain, then win through the handshake.

🤝 3-Player Handshake — When exactly 3 players remain with at least 1 independent: 2 minutes of free talk, each picks an ally, the allied pair wins. If independent is in the pair → independent wins. If mafia+citizen without independent → mafia wins.

🔄 3. Game Phases

1️⃣ Setup: Players are added, roles are selected and assigned (minimum 8 players).

2️⃣ Role Reveal: Each player sees their role in order.

3️⃣ Blind Day (1 minute): Free discussion, no voting or challenges.

4️⃣ Blind Night: Only mafia wakes up and sees each other. Jack also places his curse.

5️⃣ Night (subsequent rounds): All roles with night actions wake up and act.

6️⃣ Day: Night results announced, discussion, noon nap (if bomb), morning shots (if bullets), then voting.

7️⃣ Game End: When any team's victory condition is met.

☀️ 5. Day Rules

📢 Night Results Announcement

God announces who was killed, saved, whose shield activated, who is silenced, and Kane's reveal.

💬 Discussion

Players discuss (default 60 seconds). Silenced players cannot speak.

🔫 Morning Shots

  • Blank bullet → target survives
  • Live bullet without protection → target eliminated, side revealed
  • Live bullet with heal/shield/block → treated as blank
  • ⚠️ Unused live bullets explode at voting start, eliminating their holders!

💣 Noon Nap (if bomb active)

Stage 1: Bodyguard guesses bomb code (correct → defused, wrong → bodyguard eliminated).
Stage 2: If no bodyguard or skips, bombed player guesses (correct → defused, wrong → eliminated).

🗳️ Voting (Two Stages)

Stage 1: Each alive player votes. Threshold = floor((alive − 1) / 2) + 1. Any player reaching the threshold advances to Stage 2.

Stage 2 (Runoff):

  • If only 1 player advanced → needs the same threshold votes to be executed
  • If 2+ players → the one with the most votes is executed
  • If votes are tied → coin flip (شیر یا خط) decides

🃏 Last Action Card: The condemned player draws and uses a Last Action card before being eliminated.

⚠️ Exception — Jack: If the chosen player is Jack → God announces he is Jack. Jack stays alive but his curse is permanently locked.

🔴 6. Mafia Team Roles

🎩 Godfather Unique

Mafia leader. Each night chooses one of three actions:

  • Shoot: Regular kill (no effect on Jack or Zodiac).
  • Salakhi: Guess exact role. Correct → eliminated, bypasses ALL protections (doctor/shield/bodyguard). Wrong → nothing. On salakhi night, mafia has no regular shot. Death NOT revivable.
  • Negotiate: Only when Negotiator alive and alive mafia ≤ threshold. Target simple citizen or suspect → joins mafia. Others → fails and mafia loses shot.

Has one-time shield. Appears as citizen in detective investigation.

💉 Dr. Lecter Unique

Each night heals one mafia member (or self). Unlimited heals on others, limited self-heal (default 2). Heal persists until morning — consumed if target is shot.

💣 Bomber Unique

Once per game, plants bomb on someone with code 1-4. God announces in morning. Result determined during noon nap.

🕵️ Spy Unique

No night action. If recruited by Freemason, joins without contamination and works for mafia.

🤐 Silencer Unique

Each night silences one player (mafia/citizen/independent). That player cannot speak next day.

🧙 Sorcerer (Jadoogar) Unique

Each night blocks one citizen or independent's night action. Cannot block same person two nights in a row. Blocked bullets become blank. Blocked Jack's curse is locked and preserved.

🤝 Negotiator Unique

No night action. Enables Godfather's negotiate option when alive mafia ≤ threshold (default 2).

🔫 Simple Mafia Up to 10

Regular member with no special abilities.

🔵 7. Citizen Team Roles

⚕️ Dr. Watson Unique

Each night heals one person (or self). Unlimited heals on others, limited self-heal (default 2). Healed target survives one lethal shot.

🔍 Detective Unique

Each night investigates one player:

  • Mafia (not Godfather) or Suspect → 👍 (positive)
  • Godfather, independent, or citizen (not Suspect) → 👎 (negative)
  • Blocked by Sorcerer → ✊ (blocked)

🎖️ Citizen Kane Unique

Once per game, selects a target. If target survives until morning and is mafia or independent → God announces their role. Target stays in game. Next night, God eliminates Kane (NOT revivable). If target dies same night → ability returns.

🎯 Sniper Unique

Limited shots (default 2). Independent target → nothing. Godfather with shield → nothing. Mafia healed → wasted. Mafia unprotected → killed. Citizen target → sniper dies. Has one-time shield.

🔫 Gunner Unique

Each night gives bullets (blank or live) to players — max one per person. Morning: holder shoots. Unused live bullets explode at voting start!

🔺 Freemason Unique

Each night recruits one person. Max 2 allies (configurable). Citizen or Spy → safe. Mafia (not spy) or independent → morning: entire freemason team eliminated (contamination).

🛡️ Bodyguard Unique

No night action. 1) Noon nap: Guesses bomb code (correct → defused, wrong → bodyguard eliminated). 2) If Zodiac shoots bodyguard → Zodiac dies, bodyguard survives.

📰 Reporter Unique

After negotiation, one chance to ask God if it succeeded. God shows 👍 or 👎. Requires Negotiator in game.

✝️ Constantine Unique

Once per game, revives one dead player. Cannot revive salakhi victims or Kane sacrifice.

👤 Simple Citizen Up to 10

No abilities.

🔎 Suspect Up to 10

No abilities. Appears as mafia in detective investigation (false positive). Recruitable via negotiation.

🟣 8. Independent Roles

🔪 Jack Unique

Each night curses one person. If cursed player is killed or voted out → Jack is also eliminated.

  • Immune to night shots (mafia/zodiac/sniper)
  • Immune to day votes
  • Cannot curse same person two nights in a row
  • Only ways to eliminate: correct salakhi, curse chain
  • If blocked by Sorcerer: curse is locked and cannot be moved
  • 🏆 Victory: If all mafia die and Jack is alive → Jack wins instantly! Also wins through the 3-player handshake.

♈ Zodiac Unique

Independent killer. Shoots based on settings (every night / odd / even).

  • Immune to night shots
  • If shoots Bodyguard → Zodiac dies, bodyguard survives
  • Can be voted out during day
  • Can be eliminated by correct salakhi
  • 🏆 Victory: Does NOT auto-win when mafia dies. Must keep killing until 3 players remain and win through the handshake endgame.

⚡ 9. Special Mechanics

🗡️ Salakhi

Godfather guesses exact role. Correct → eliminated, bypasses all protection. Wrong → nothing. On salakhi night, mafia has no shot. Death NOT revivable. Can kill Jack and Zodiac.

🤝 Negotiation

When alive mafia ≤ threshold and Negotiator alive. Only simple citizen or suspect can be recruited. Any other role → fails, mafia loses shot.

💣 Bomb

Bomber plants once with code 1-4. Morning: announced. Noon nap: Bodyguard → bombed player guess.

🔪 Jack's Curse

Each night on one person. If that person dies → Jack dies too. Sorcerer block → curse locked.

🛡️ Shield

Godfather and Sniper each have one-time shield. Absorbs one night shot. Salakhi/bomb/vote/bullet explosion bypass shield.

🔺 Freemason Alliance

Each night recruits one. Citizen/spy → safe. Mafia/independent → contamination (entire team eliminated next morning). If leader dies → alliance inactive.

🔫 Morning Shots & Bullets

Gunner gives bullets at night. Morning: holder shoots. Blank = safe. Live = lethal if unprotected. Unused live = explosion at voting.

🤝 3-Player Handshake Endgame

When exactly 3 players remain with at least 1 independent, the game enters the handshake phase: 2 minutes of free talk, then each player picks who to ally with. The allied pair is the winner. If an independent is in the pair → independent wins. If mafia+citizen without independent → mafia wins. If 2 citizens → citizen wins.

🃏 10. Last Action Cards

When a player is eliminated by vote, they choose a number from 1 to the number of remaining cards and draw a random card. Each game has 5 cards, and each card can only be used once.

🔫 1. Final Shoot

  • The eliminated player immediately chooses an alive player and shoots them
  • If the target is shoot-immune (Jack, Zodiac, Godfather), the shot has no effect
  • If the target was healed by Watson, the shot has no effect
  • If successful, the target dies immediately
  • Side effect: Mafia loses their regular shoot the following night

🌙 2. Insomnia

  • The next night is completely skipped
  • No night actions occur (no mafia shoot, no Watson heal, no detective inquiry, etc.)
  • The game goes directly to the next day phase
  • This is automatic — no target selection needed

👁️ 3. Reveal Identity

  • God announces the eliminated player's true role to all remaining players
  • This is automatic — no target selection needed
  • Side effect: The eliminated player can no longer be revived by Constantine

🧠 4. Beautiful Mind

  • The eliminated player chooses an alive player and guesses they are an independent (Jack or Zodiac)
  • If the guess is correct (target is independent), the target is eliminated
  • Bonus: If the guess is correct, the voted-out player is revived and returns to the game!
  • If the guess is wrong, nothing happens

🎭 5. Face Off

  • The eliminated player chooses an alive player
  • The eliminated player's role is fully transferred to the chosen player (including shield and abilities)
  • The chosen player continues the game with this new role
  • Side effect: The eliminated player can no longer be revived

⚙️ 11. Setup Rules & Configuration

Constraints

  • Minimum 8 players
  • At least 1 Mafia role required
  • Max mafia: floor((total players − independents − 1) / 2)
  • Zodiac requires Bodyguard (auto-added)
  • Reporter requires Negotiator
  • Role count must exactly match player count
